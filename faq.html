What does 579999 mean?

Read in Mandarin, it sounds like 我妻久久久久 ('my wife, forever and ever') or 吾起久久久久 ('I rise, lasting forever'). 9999 alone means 'forever' because 9 (jiǔ) sounds like 久 (lasting).

Why is 8 lucky and 4 unlucky in Chinese?

8 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā, to prosper), and 4 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ, death).

Is the luck score scientific?

No. It measures how Chinese speakers traditionally perceive a number, based on homophones and patterns. It is cultural, not predictive.
