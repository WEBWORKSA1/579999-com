# 579999.com — Research Brief & Business Case

_Compiled 30 Sep 2026._

## 1. What "579999" means

| Layer | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Mandarin homophones** | 5 五 wǔ ≈ 我/吾 ("I, me"); 7 七 qī ≈ 妻 ("wife"), 起 ("rise"), 气 ("vital energy"); 9 九 jiǔ ≈ 久 ("long-lasting"). |
| **Phrase readings** | **我妻久久久久** "My wife, forever and ever" (love/devotion; the same family as 520 and 1314). **吾起久久久久** "I rise, lasting forever" (ambition, enduring success). |
| **Honest caveat** | 579999 is **not** a fixed idiom like 1314. Its meaning is built from standard homophones, which is how most lucky numbers get theirs. |
| **Cantonese** | 5 (ng5) ≈ 唔 "not", and 7 and 9 have vulgar slang senses, so the reading is weaker for HK and Guangdong buyers. The AAAA pattern is still valued in every dialect. |
| **Culture** | 9 was the Emperor's number (nine-dragon robes, 9×9 door studs). It is used for weddings (99/999 roses, 3399 长长久久 red envelopes) and longevity. |
| **Economy: gold** | "9999" = 999.9 fine ("four nines") gold, the top commercial purity grade. Chinese marks 足金 / 千足金. |
| **Economy: numbers market** | AAAA endings are the top pricing tier for phones and plates. 579999 has **no 0 and no 4**, so it belongs to the 262,144-combo "premium 6N .com" class that Chinese investors bought up in 2015–16 (NamePros). |

### Market evidence (sourced)
- Hong Kong plate **28**: HK$18.1M (≈US$2.3M), 2016. Earlier records: 18 ≈ US$2.1M, 9 ≈ US$1.7M. (Bloomberg)
- **+86 28 8888 8888**: CN¥2.33M (≈US$280k), bought by Sichuan Airlines in 2003. (Wikipedia, Chinese numerology)
- Phone number ending in five 5s: CN¥350k+ (≈US$52k), 30× its opening bid, 107 bids, 2019 Alibaba court auction. (SCMP)
- 6N .com prices in 2016: $160 random, $260–$2,800+ for patterns. 888733.com sold for £2,808. (NamePros)
- 88888@88.com numeric email address: US$24,000. (Domain Name Wire, 2020)

## 2. Chosen idea (and why)

**579999.com = "Lucky Number Exchange": decode, value and trade Chinese lucky numbers.**

Three ideas were compared:

| Idea | Traffic | RPM | Lead value | Differentiation | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generic "lucky numbers meaning" content site | High | Low (culture niche) | Low | None; crowded (chinahighlights, travelchinaguide) | ✗ |
| Love-code site ("my wife forever") | Medium, seasonal (5/20, Qixi) | Low–med | Low | Overlaps sister love-code properties | ✗ |
| **Lucky Number Exchange: free appraiser/decoder tools + buy/sell/appraise desk** | High (tool queries: "is my phone number lucky", "what does 520 mean") | Medium (finance/auto/telecom/jewellery advertisers) | **High** (buyers/sellers of $500–$50k+ assets) | Few sites combine culture tools **and** a transaction desk | ✓ |

The logic: tools pull search traffic that earns AdSense, and every tool result ends in a high-intent call to action ("get expert appraisal / list it / source it"). Revenue then comes from four streams: ads, brokerage commissions and lead sales, consult fees, and sponsorships and donations. The domain itself (6 digits, no 0/4, quad-9) is the live proof of concept.
